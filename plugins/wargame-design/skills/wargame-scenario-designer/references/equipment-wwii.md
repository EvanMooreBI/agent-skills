# WWII Equipment Reference

**Important:** The weapon lists below show correct naming conventions, not TO&E compositions. Before writing any OOB, research the actual Table of Organization and Equipment for the specific nation, branch, year, and theater via web search. Squad sizes, weapon distributions per fire team, and support weapon allocation differ significantly between (e.g.) a 1942 British rifle section in North Africa and a 1944 British rifle section in Normandy.

## Skirmish Scale: Individual Weapons

**Rifles:** Lee-Enfield No.4, M1 Garand, Kar98k, Mosin-Nagant M91/30, Arisaka Type 99
**SMGs:** Sten Mk II, Thompson M1A1, MP40, PPSh-41, Type 100
**Assault Rifles:** StG 44
**LMGs:** Bren Mk I, BAR M1918A2, MG 42 (bipod), DP-28
**Pistols:** Webley Mk VI, M1911A1, Walther P38, Nagant M1895
**Shotguns:** Winchester M1897 Trench Gun
**Anti-tank:** PIAT, Bazooka M1A1, Panzerfaust 60, Panzerschreck (RPzB 54)

**Support Weapons:**
- Light Mortar: 2-inch Mortar, M2 60mm Mortar, 5cm leGrW 36
- Medium MG: Vickers Mk I, M1919A4 .30 cal, MG 42 (tripod)
- HMG: M2 .50 cal, DShK 1938
- Flamethrower: M2 Flamethrower, Flammenwerfer 41

## Platoon Scale: Squad Weapon Examples

**British Rifle Platoon (Bolt Action):**
```
1x Platoon HQ: Lieutenant + 1 Rifleman (Lee-Enfield)
3x Rifle Section (10 men): NCO (Sten), Bren Gun team (Bren Mk I + loader), 8 Riflemen (Lee-Enfield)
1x 2-inch Mortar team (2 men)
1x PIAT team (2 men)
```

**German Panzergrenadier Platoon:**
```
1x Platoon HQ: Leutnant + 2 men (MP40, Kar98k)
3x Panzergrenadier Squad (10 men): NCO (MP40), MG 42 team (gunner + loader), 7 Riflemen (Kar98k)
  — Each squad carries 1x Panzerfaust
1x Sdkfz 251/1 Hanomag per squad (transport)
```

## Company/Battalion Scale: FOW-Style Team Types

At company scale and above, individual models are grouped onto bases/stands representing teams. Use **Flames of War team type naming conventions** — the industry standard for multi-based 15mm and 6mm wargaming.

### Infantry Team Types

| Team Type | Description | Typical Base |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Rifle team** | Basic infantry armed with rifles | Medium (32mm x 50mm) |
| **Rifle/MG team** | Mixed team with rifles and one LMG (e.g., BAR, Bren) | Medium |
| **MG team** | Team with significant MG firepower (every team has a machine-gun) | Medium |
| **SMG team** | Team armed entirely with submachine-guns | Medium |
| **Assault Rifle team** | Team armed with assault rifles (e.g., StG 44) | Medium |
| **Pioneer/Engineer team** | Combat engineers; may carry demolition charges, flamethrowers | Medium |
| **Scout/Recce team** | Reconnaissance infantry; typically smaller team | Small |

### Anti-Tank Teams

| Team Type | Description | Typical Base |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Bazooka team** | US M1/M9 Bazooka (2 crew) | Small (25mm x 32mm) |
| **PIAT team** | British Projector, Infantry, Anti-Tank (2 crew) | Small |
| **Panzerschreck team** | German RPzB 54 (2 crew) | Small |
| **Panzerfaust team** | German single-use AT weapon (often integrated into Rifle teams) | — |

### Heavy Weapon Teams (Man-Packed Guns)

| Team Type | Description | Typical Base |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **HMG team** | Heavy machine-gun on tripod (e.g., Vickers, M1917, MG 42 on Lafette) | Medium or Large |
| **Light Mortar team** | Man-portable mortar (e.g., 2-inch, 60mm M2) | Small |
| **Medium Mortar team** | Heavier mortar (e.g., 3-inch, 81mm, 8cm GrW 34) | Medium |
| **Heavy Mortar team** | Large mortar (e.g., 4.2-inch, 120mm PM-38) | Large |
| **Flame-thrower team** | Portable flamethrower (2 crew) | Small |
| **Sniper team** | Sniper + observer (2 crew) | Small |

### Gun Teams (Crew-Served Weapons on Bases)

| Team Type | Description | Typical Base |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Anti-Tank Gun** | Towed AT gun + crew. Always name the specific gun: "6-pdr Anti-Tank Gun", "7.5cm PaK 40", "57mm ZiS-2", "3-inch M5 Gun" | Large (50mm x 65mm) |
| **Infantry Gun** | Light direct-support gun: "7.5cm leIG 18", "76mm M1927 Regimental Gun" | Large |
| **Field Artillery** | Indirect fire gun: "25-pdr Field Gun", "10.5cm leFH 18", "76mm ZiS-3", "105mm M2A1 Howitzer" | Large |
| **Medium/Heavy Artillery** | Larger artillery: "5.5-inch Medium Gun", "15cm sFH 18", "122mm A-19", "155mm M1 Long Tom" | Large |
| **AA Gun** | Anti-aircraft: "Bofors 40mm", "2cm FlaK 38", "3.7cm FlaK 43", "M16 MGMC" | Large |
| **Dual-purpose Gun** | AT and AA capable: "8.8cm FlaK 36" | Large |
| **Rocket Launcher** | "15cm Nebelwerfer 41", "BM-13 Katyusha", "T34 Calliope" | Large |

### Command and Specialist Teams

| Team Type | Description | Typical Base |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Command team** | Company or platoon commander + runner/radio operator | Small |
| **2iC Command team** | Second-in-command | Small |
| **Observer team** | Forward observer for artillery (FOO/FO) | Small |
| **Staff team** | Higher HQ element | Small |
| **Medic/Aid team** | Medical personnel | Small |
| **Messenger team** | Runner or dispatch rider | Small |

### Transport

Always specify the vehicle model:
**Universal Carrier** (Bren Carrier), **M3 Half-track**, **Sdkfz 251/1 Hanomag**, **Opel Blitz**, **Bedford QL**, **Studebaker US6**, **GAZ-67**, **Jeep (Willys MB)**, **Kübelwagen**, **Sdkfz 10/11**
