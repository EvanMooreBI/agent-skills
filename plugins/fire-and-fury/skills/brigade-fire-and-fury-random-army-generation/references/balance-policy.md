# Force balance review

The generator records descriptive measures for both forces before publication:

- real and display-only corps and divisions;
- infantry and cavalry brigade counts and stands;
- battery counts;
- exceptional leader counts;
- experience, morale, and weapon distributions.

The review must identify conspicuous differences, but it must not score, reroll, add, remove, or change a unit. No approved rule defines how these measures combine into a balance score or which adjustments preserve the source generator's intent.

Every generation log must contain this decision:

`No adjustment applied: balance rules not yet approved.`

Future work must define thresholds, permitted adjustments, and acceptance tests before automatic balancing is enabled.
