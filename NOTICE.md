## Third-party material

This repository contains no rulebook text, scenarios, maps, quick-reference sheets, articles, or fonts owned by others. The scenario-format examples describe page layouts in original words.

The Fire and Fury skills include two kinds of rules data as play aids. The first is the Brigade Fire and Fury and Regimental Fire and Fury brigade effectiveness tables, which are of no use without the rules. The second is the Regimental skill's army-generation data. The Brigade Fire and Fury army-generator tables are not included. Users build them from photos of their own copy of the rulebook.

Skills that can use material you own list it in their `local-resources.json`. You supply those files yourself and keep them outside this repository. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#local-resources).
